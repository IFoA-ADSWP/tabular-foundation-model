# Plan — the runs ledger says how much data existed, not only how much was trained on

**Status:** Proposal — issue #671 (parent #668, the cross-task ledger).
**Scope:** the generator, its CSV and document outputs, its tests, and the CSV's column reference.
No capture is touched, no metric is invented, and no verdict moves.

## Why

The ledger shows `train_rows`, so a reader can see that a fair-test arm trained on 10,000 rows
and a full-pool baseline on 542,410. It cannot show that the French frequency dataset holds
678,013 policies, so the first row is 1.5% of the available data and the second is 80%. A
training budget is only interpretable against the size of the dataset it was drawn from.

## What changes

- `scripts/analyses/finetuning_runs_ledger.py`
  - a curated `DATASET_SIZES` map: dataset → eligible rows, unit and source pointer, declared as
    **curated from the pre-registrations, not derived from captures**;
  - two new CSV columns, `eligible_rows` and `share_of_eligible`, rendered per row;
  - a refusal when a dataset in the ledger has no curated size, so a new dataset cannot silently
    render blank;
  - the document's dataset table gains eligible rows and unit, and its ledger table gains both
    columns; one sentence says the count is curated and why the share is per row.
- `docs/analyses/data/finetuning_runs_ledger.csv` and
  `docs/experiments/pilot2-phase2/FINETUNING_RUNS_LEDGER.md` — regenerated by the script, never
  hand-edited.
- `docs/analyses/data/README.md` — the CSV's column reference gains the two columns.
- `tests/test_finetuning_runs_ledger.py` — coverage refusal, the pinned shares, and that the
  share is computed rather than stored.

## Decisions

1. **The counts are curated, and the source travels with each one.** They come from the
   pre-registrations' own dataset tables — `FINETUNING_EXPERIMENT_REFERENCE.md`,
   `regime_characterization.md` and the frontier dataset table, and
   `REGRESSOR_FINETUNING_PREREGISTRATION_FREQ_SEV_2026-11.md` — not from a capture and not from a
   count of the committed records. A capture covers the arms that ran; the eligible size is the
   prepared dataset, which no capture records. The map stores the source pointer so a reader can
   check a number without re-deriving it.

2. **The share is computed per row, not per dataset.** The same dataset has a 10,000-row TabPFN
   row and a 542,410-row full-pool baseline row; one share per dataset would be wrong for one of
   them. `share_of_eligible = train_rows / eligible_rows`, formatted to four decimal places.

3. **The eligible count is the one in the ledger's own unit.** `freMTPL2sev`'s rows are claims
   (the full fold pool is ~21,150 claims), so its eligible count is 26,444 claims, beside the
   24,944 policies that carry them. `spanish_motor_severity`'s rows are claiming policies
   (4,760 per fold pool), so its count is 5,950 claiming policies, beside the 11,167
   claim-equivalents. Pairing a claim count with a policy-row numerator would produce a share
   above 100% or a meaningless one.

4. **A blank numerator gives a blank share, and the eligible count stays.** The Spanish
   `no-capture` breadth rows have no `train_rows` — the breadth result owns that number and the
   per-seed records were never committed — so their share is blank. Their `eligible_rows` is not
   blank: it is a property of the dataset (53,502 policies), curated and sourced independently of
   whether a capture exists. A blank share says "the numerator is unknown", not "the dataset has
   no size".

5. **A dataset with no curated size fails the generator.** The renderer looks the dataset up in
   `DATASET_SIZES` and raises rather than printing an empty cell, so adding a campaign to the
   registry without a sourced size is a refusal, not a silently blank column.

## Verification

```
PATH="$PWD/.venv/bin:$PATH" ./scripts/check.sh
```

The committed-artefact byte compare (`test_the_committed_artefacts_are_byte_identical_to_a_fresh_render`)
covers the regenerated CSV and Markdown. The new tests pin the three known shares
(freMTPL2freq 10,000 → 0.0147; freMTPL2freq 542,410 → 0.8000; spanish_motor_severity
4,760 → 0.8000), assert every dataset in the ledger has an entry, and prove the share moves with
a fabricated `train_rows`.
